# Figures Folder

Store figure assets or figure-generation outputs here.

Do not store conceptual figure definitions here. The only source of truth for figure meaning is `PROJECT_SPEC.md`.

Before creating a figure, check `PROJECT_SPEC.md` Section 12 for:
- purpose,
- required data,
- section placement,
- style notes,
- color and accessibility constraints,
- and the detailed figure-spec layer for structure, semantics, layout, and caption requirements.

Do not implement a figure unless the matching `11A` entry is complete enough to derive it without invention.

Placeholder snippets such as `figure_1_placeholder.tex` are allowed here, but they must only point back to `PROJECT_SPEC.md` and must not introduce new figure semantics.
