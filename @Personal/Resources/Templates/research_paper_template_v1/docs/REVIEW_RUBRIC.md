# Review Rubric

Evaluate the manuscript on the following dimensions.

## 1. Problem Framing
- Is the problem clear?
- Is the gap specific?
- Is the motivation credible and not overstated?

## 2. Contribution Framing
- Are the contributions concrete?
- Are they distinct?
- Are they appropriately scoped?

## 3. Technical Soundness
- Are definitions clear?
- Is notation consistent?
- Are assumptions explicit?
- Are proofs/results labeled accurately?

## 4. Evidence Integrity
- Are claims matched to evidence?
- Are experiments real rather than implied?
- Are failure cases and limits acknowledged?
- Are proof and result requirements respected section by section?

## 5. Related Work
- Does it fairly position itself?
- Does it avoid straw-manning?
- Are key rivals or foundational works missing?

## 6. Writing Quality
- Logical flow
- section transitions
- readability
- sentence discipline
- jargon control

## 7. Formatting and Build Quality
- modular LaTeX structure
- working cross-references
- bibliography consistency
- no dangling placeholders near submission
- consistency with the requested PDF style target
- consistency between figures and the figure-spec layer in `PROJECT_SPEC.md`

## 8. Reviewer Risk
- likely attacks
- overclaims
- unsupported novelty language
- missing limitations
